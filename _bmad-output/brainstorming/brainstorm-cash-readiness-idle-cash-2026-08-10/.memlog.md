- (technique) started First Principles Thinking
- (idea) Bedrock property of a dollar: how fast I can actually spend/deploy it (liquidity / time-to-trade)
- (idea) Bedrock property of a dollar: whether it's earning yield
- (idea) Third bedrock property lives in the USER not the dollar: the reserve — how much they never want to touch; varies by personal risk threshold (all-in vs keep a buffer if income dries up)
- (insight) Complication: the reserve is partly about money OUTSIDE Schwab (bank savings) the app can't see — so Ballast can never fully know the true safety picture
- (idea) Reserve is OPTIONAL, never required; user is responsible for what the app can't see
- (idea) Allow a zero reserve — some users keep their entire buffer in their bank, outside Schwab, so 0 in-app reserve is legitimate
- (idea) Missed-growth base = settlement cash + user-specified funds (e.g. money market), not just settlement cash
- (idea) When a reserve is set, subtract it from cash + specified funds before computing missed growth
- (idea) Require a first-run step where the user explicitly SETS or DECLINES a reserve — so an empty reserve is an intentional choice, never a silent assumption (resolves the honesty concern)
- (decision) After the user has explicitly set-or-declined, it's fair to treat an unset reserve as intended-empty (0)
- (idea) Reserve amount must be editable later (a way to change it)
- (decision) Reserve comes out of money market FIRST; keep settlement cash liquid for trading (MMF still earns yield + is liquid enough for emergencies; don't waste settlement cash holding a buffer at 0%)
- (idea) Keep v1 simple: compute missed growth from reserve + settlement cash + user-specified money-market funds; and just a NOTIFICATION when you'd need to liquidate money market to fund a purchase — not a full automated liquidation flow
- (idea) At the shortfall moment, app PRE-POPULATES the money-market liquidation (sell) order form with the exact needed amount, then stops and waits for the user to review + submit — propose-and-approve, same pattern as the existing 8.4 'suggest & populate the order' button
- (idea) Yield-aware missed growth: a money-market dollar misses LESS than a settlement-cash dollar because it earns yield → settlement cash = full benchmark return; money market = (benchmark return − its yield)
- (technique) started Reverse Brainstorming
- (idea) FAILURE MODE: scaring the user into dumping their whole reserve into the market — the missed-growth number weaponized into FOMO/fear
- (idea) FAILURE MODE: showing stale prices (e.g. from before a market crash) → missed-growth/portfolio numbers silently wrong, misleading while sounding perfectly calm
- (decision) Liquidation step triggers ONLY when a purchase is decided AND settlement cash is insufficient (just-in-time at the buy step, never a proactive 'go liquidate' nudge)
- (idea) FAILURE MODE: the money-market sell might not settle in time to place the buy — a settlement-timing gap leaves the user stranded between the liquidation and the purchase
- (idea) SAFEGUARD: hard rule — never place the buy without settled funds available
- (idea) SAFEGUARD: notification system (web-only OK for v1) that alerts the user when the money-market sell has settled
- (idea) SAFEGUARD: attach the original intended buy to the settlement notification (remember what they wanted to buy)
- (idea) SAFEGUARD: click handler on the notification → navigates to buy page, pre-populates the controls, waits for user to submit (deferred/resumed buy flow)
- (idea) SAFEGUARD (FOMO inverse): CAP the nudge — never nudge on the full amount; bound how aggressive the suggestion can be
- (idea) SAFEGUARD (FOMO inverse): ALWAYS frame missed-growth against the reserve — compute only on investable (post-reserve) money and show the protected reserve alongside so it never reads as 'invest everything'
- (insight) SYNTHESIS / chosen v1 direction: Model cash as THREE states — ready-to-trade (settlement cash), parked (user-specified money-market funds), reserved (user-declared, optional, editable). Honesty rule: explicit set-or-decline reserve up front, never assume. Missed-growth = yield-aware (settlement cash misses full benchmark; MMF misses benchmark minus its yield) and computed only on investable = (cash + specified MMF − reserve), with reserve taken from MMF first and the protected reserve shown alongside; nudges CAPPED (no FOMO). Deploy flow: just-in-time only (at buy step when settlement cash short) — app pre-populates the MMF liquidation sell (propose-and-approve, like 8.4), never places a buy without settled funds; on settlement, a durable 'pending buy' card + notification resumes the original buy pre-filled for user submit. Villains to guard: FOMO, stale prices, settlement-timing gap. Out of v1: auto-classify tickers, external/bank cash, automated liquidation.
