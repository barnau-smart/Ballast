- (technique) started Reverse Brainstorming
- (idea) Scariest failure modes to defend against: #1 the AI confidently states a made-up number/analysis; #2 it teaches a bad habit BY EXAMPLE (user internalizes it as 'how investing works'); #4 a prescribed move triggers a tax hit (wash sale / short-term gains).
- (decision) DESIGN DECISION: over-automation / 'user never actually learns' is NOT a failure — using it as a permanent tool without ever graduating is an ACCEPTABLE use case. Learning/graduation is OPTIONAL; the product is tool-first, teacher-optionally.
- (idea) SAFEGUARD (inverse of #1): 'don't invent anything.' Every number/fact in an action item is DETERMINISTICALLY computed by code; the AI only narrates it; a validator rejects any figure the AI states that wasn't handed to it. Reuses the EXISTING Precedent-Engine + evidence-record + validation-gate architecture, pointed at the portfolio-analysis engine — not a new safety model.
- (idea) Inverse of #2 (MasterB): example-moves are grounded in the deterministic portfolio analysis + real market data, NOT the AI's opinion. Wants the fiduciary-advisor persona to define good-lesson vs bad-habit.
- (idea) SAFEGUARD (inverse of #2, advisor persona) — 5 tests every demonstrated move must pass to teach good judgment not bad habits: (1) it's a PRINCIPLE not a pick (user could re-derive it); (2) the 'why' GENERALIZES (works every time, not just once — filters out chasing); (3) it's a RECOGNIZED best practice / fiduciary consensus, not the app's private opinion; (4) it teaches the TRADEOFF + counter-case (reuse the existing required 'uncertainties'); (5) market data informs the FACTS never the FORECAST. Underlying line: demonstrate timeless principles applied to real numbers, never market calls.
- (note) CORRECTION (MasterB pushed back, coach conceded): scratch 'grounded in analysis NOT the AI's opinion' — that over-constrained it. The real line: the AI must never invent a FACT/NUMBER (hallucinated figures = a lie the user acts on), BUT its OPINION/JUDGMENT on the real numbers is exactly the value of an expert agent and IS wanted (prioritize, synthesize, take a point of view). Numbers = deterministic; judgment on them = welcome AI opinion.
- (decision) LOCKED: the AI expert opines on the USER'S SITUATION + sound principles ONLY (concentration, cash drag, diversification gaps, cost, tax, rebalancing) — it does NOT make market forecasts/predictions. Honest-limit: it lacks full tax context (bracket, other accounts) -> flags 'there may be a tax angle, worth a pro' rather than faking certainty. Tax-aware, not tax-omniscient.
