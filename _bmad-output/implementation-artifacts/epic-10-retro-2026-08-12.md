## What went well

- **The Epic 9 lesson paid off within one epic.** Action item #1 ("mandatory independent review before merging loop money-path work") was applied and immediately caught a HIGH money bug. The process proved its value concretely — MasterB's top-cited win alongside the two below.
- **All cardinal money-safety contracts verified CLEAN end-to-end** (Group C): populate-don't-submit (no order placed / no `decision_record` written from any read endpoint or the frontend), per-user scoping (AD-10), no XSS (React-escaped, no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`), fixed-point money serialization. The loop built a fundamentally safe surface — the bugs were in the *math and honesty*, not the safety rails.
- **Fast delivery with the guardrails genuinely enforced in code** — never-invent-a-fact numeric gate, no-forecast denylist, nothing-to-do-is-valid (empty findings → no LLM call), rebalance-not-chase (index-core/parked excluded, never-past-target caps). Not merely asserted in comments — verified by all three review layers.

## Challenges & lessons

- **The loop's self-review actively REJECTED a real HIGH money bug.** `spec-10-2`'s own rejected-findings list contained "parked-vs-cash model (established Epic 9)" — the exact bug the independent review caught: the deploy engine subtracted parked money-market *holdings* from *settlement* cash, under-deploying or falsely reporting `no_cash` for any user of the Epic 9 park feature (fixed `fa3ecf6`, aligned to Epic 9's canonical `ready_to_trade = view.cash`). **The loop rationalizes wrong money behavior into its own "rejected" list — its "reviewed" stamp is not evidence for money-path work.**
- **A duplicate-symbol double-sell oversell was in NO spec** (Group A, fixed `0ff200f`) — two cache rows of the same ticker each cleared the concentration ceiling at an understated weight and surfaced as two overlapping SELLs a human could co-sign into an oversell. Found independently by two review layers.
- **Traceability / spec-hygiene gap:** Story 10-1 shipped with **no governing spec file**, and `expense_ratio.py` (a Story 10-4 file) was bundled into earlier commits. Harder to audit against intent.
- **Money-accounting ambiguity is a recurring risk** — the parked-cash bug sits in the same area as Epic 9 action item #2(a) (confirm Schwab `portfolio_balance.cash` is settled-only). The deploy engine now *depends* on `view.cash = settlement`, so that confirmation matters more.
- **Guardrail backstops are porous by design** — the no-forecast denylist missed modal-hedge predictions ("could double"/"may rise", fixed `0ff200f`) and the numeric gate is value/unit-blind (accepted residual → promoted to Story 10-6).
