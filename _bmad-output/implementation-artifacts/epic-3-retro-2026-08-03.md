Epic 3 built the deterministic precedent backbone of Ballast — the sole, provable source of
every market fact the coach may ever cite — plus the three calming pull-only views that defuse
scary moments. Originally 3.1–3.5; **reopened 2026-08-03 to add Story 3.6** (hypothetical-drawdown
precedent) after an FR20 intent gap surfaced.

## What went well

1. **The structural-teeth invariant was born here.** AD-3 (the Precedent Engine is the *single*
   source of market statistics) + AD-12 (every evidence record is the fixed 6-field shape) made
   "an LLM can never fabricate a market number" un-bypassable at a seam. Epic 4's retrospective
   explicitly credits this pattern for *ten stories with zero high-severity findings*. Epic 3 is
   where that discipline originated.
2. **Determinism held end to end.** Same inputs → same matched windows and byte-stable IDs, no LLM
   in the matching path. Story 3.6 extended this property to hypothetical queries (byte-stable,
   magnitude-encoded IDs) without touching the contract.
3. **Additive-only reopening.** 3.6 added a full hypothetical-drawdown capability with the AD-12
   6-field contract preserved — `stats.hypothetical` / `stats.hypothetical_drawdown_pct` are safe
   additive keys — so nothing rippled into the Epic 4 coach/replay that depend on the exact shape.
4. **Never-a-dead-end honesty.** No band match degrades to the `strategy` default with an honest
   `no_band_match` reason (target encoded for a stable id) — never empty, never an error.

## What was hard / what we learned

1. **A "done" epic missed part of its FR intent.** Original Epic 3 shipped 3.5 as done, but FR20's
   real intent — *fear usually strikes before or during a drop, not only after* — went unserved:
   with the market near a high, the contextualizer could only ever return a shallow-dip precedent,
   even though the data holds 2008/2020/2022. That is why 3.6 had to reopen the epic. **This is the
   same failure mode as Epic 4's missing coach-card centerpiece** — acceptance validated the ACs but
   not the full requirement intent. Recurring pattern → guardrail (AI-3-3).
2. **The honesty invariant is subtle and load-bearing.** 3.6's review caught a *high-severity*
   defect in the shared `PrecedentEvidence` renderer: a hypothetical target ("if it fell ~20%") was
   rendered as the symbol's *real current standing* — a fiction presented as fact, exactly what
   NFR8/FR20/AC5 forbid. Fixed (guarded on `stats.hypothetical`) and pinned by a test. Note the
   trade-off: the same seam that makes honesty un-bypassable also concentrates risk into one shared
   component (both precedent surfaces + the Epic 4 coach render through it), so a follow-up review
   pass was warranted and ran ("exclude current in-progress drop from hypothetical match").
3. **Process scare (2026-08-03):** the bmad-loop *looked* killed (uncommitted 3.6 tree,
   `status: in-review`, sprint `ready-for-dev`), but a live `bmad-dev-auto` session + the `cli run`
   orchestrator were actively finalizing it. A hand-finalize/commit nearly raced the live process;
   the edits only failed thanks to the "file modified since read" guard. Root cause: no liveness
   check. Captured as a memory guardrail (AI-3-2).
